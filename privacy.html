Privacy policy

Drive Connector · last updated 7 October 2026

Drive Connector is a personal tool used by one person, on their own computer.

  • What it accesses: only Google Drive folders that have been explicitly shared with the account it signs in as. It lists, reads and saves files in those folders.
  • Where data goes: files move only between Google Drive and the owner’s own computer. No data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or third party.
  • Sign-in credentials: stored only on the owner’s computer, readable only by the owner.
  • No tracking: no analytics, advertising, or selling of data. This website sets no cookies.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or stop sharing the folders.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
